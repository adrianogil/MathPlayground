The rule

Move the diagonal one place left

  1. Outside: multiply the active digit by the multiplier’s units digit.
  2. Inside: multiply its right-hand neighbor by the multiplier’s tens digit.
  3. Carry: add the incoming carry, write the units digit, and carry the rest.

The leading and edge zeros keep the same rule valid at both ends. The result is ordinary place-value multiplication reorganized by output column.
